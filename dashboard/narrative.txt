Platform Health Summary:

API platform health is currently critical with an overall error rate of 8.16% across 98 requests from 40 daily active users on November 11, 2026. This represents a deterioration from the previous day, November 10, when the error rate stood at 6.57% across 1553 requests and 494 daily active users, despite traffic dropping significantly. Average latency remained stable at 24.2ms compared to 22.8ms, though p95 latency increased from 61.6ms to 79.7ms alongside a 1.02% rate-limiting rate and 117,324 tokens processed.

The failures are heavily concentrated in specific segments, driven by three TimeoutErrors, two AuthenticationErrors, two InternalServerErrors, and one APIError. The issues predominantly impact the enterprise and pro user tiers, which suffered 60.0% and 13.64% error rates respectively, while free users experienced a healthy 2.82% error rate. Geographically, us-west and us-east bore the brunt with error rates of 23.08% and 10.0%, and endpoint degradation was severe on /v1/chat, which recorded a 16.67% error rate and a spiked p95 latency of 173.5ms.

Immediate remediation should start with investigating and resolving the root causes of the TimeoutErrors and InternalServerErrors affecting the /v1/chat endpoint, particularly for enterprise and pro tier clients in the us-west and us-east regions. Following that, engineering must audit the authentication pathways to address the two AuthenticationErrors and review capacity limits in us-west to mitigate the 23.08% failure rate. Finally, monitoring should be established to track the recovery of p95 latency on high-impact endpoints and verify that rate-limiting thresholds are appropriately tuned for the affected user tiers.
