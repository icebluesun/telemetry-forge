Platform Health Summary:

Platform status is critical as error rates remain elevated for the second consecutive day. On November 24, 2026, the API handled 807 requests from 232 daily active users with an error rate of 6.82 percent, up slightly from 6.49 percent on November 23 when the platform served 1,541 requests and 426 daily active users. Performance metrics remained steady day-over-day with an average latency of 22.3 milliseconds and a p95 latency of 66.4 milliseconds, alongside 2.6 percent rate-limited requests and 862,196 processed tokens.

Failures are heavily concentrated in specific segments, driven primarily by 13 InternalServerError instances, 11 TimeoutErrors, and 9 APIErrors, supplemented by InvalidRequestErrors and RateLimitErrors. Regionally, us-west is experiencing the worst stability with a 10.91 percent error rate, followed closely by us-east at 8.33 percent and eu-west at 6.69 percent, while ap-southeast remains exceptionally healthy at 0.78 percent. By endpoint, embeddings leads with an 8.33 percent error rate, followed by classify at 7.55 percent, completions at 6.42 percent, and chat at 6.18 percent, which also exhibits the highest p95 latency at 94.5 milliseconds. Enterprise and pro user tiers are disproportionately impacted with error rates of 9.76 percent and 9.68 percent respectively, compared to 5.69 percent for free users, suggesting upstream timeout issues or resource contention affecting high-tier payload limits, particularly given enterprise p95 latency reaching 157.0 milliseconds.

Immediate remediation must begin with an urgent investigation into the us-west and us-east infrastructure to isolate the root cause of the regional spikes in InternalServerErrors and Timeouts. Next, engineering should review backend resource allocations for the enterprise and pro tiers to address the severe latency and error disparities affecting high-value customers on the chat and embeddings endpoints. Finally, capacity planning teams need to evaluate the 2.6 percent rate-limiting threshold against incoming traffic patterns to prevent cascading failures and reduce the frequency of RateLimitErrors across all regions.
