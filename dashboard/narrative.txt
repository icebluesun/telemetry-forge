Platform Health Summary:

API platform health is currently critical as the error rate sits at 6.83 percent, continuing an ongoing stability issue following yesterday's 7.14 percent error rate. Across 834 total requests from 273 daily active users, performance held relatively steady with an average latency of 22.0 milliseconds and a p95 latency of 59.9 milliseconds, alongside 2.4 percent rate-limited requests and 926,854 processed tokens. This represents a significant drop in traffic volume from yesterday's 1,569 requests and 530 daily active users, even as the high error rate persisted.

The platform failures are heavily concentrated in the enterprise tier, which suffered a 25.0 percent error rate and a spiked p95 latency of 257.3 milliseconds, as well as the us-west and us-east regions with 11.93 percent and 9.17 percent error rates respectively. Among endpoints, embeddings and completions performed worst at 8.07 percent and 6.98 percent error rates. The primary failure drivers are internal server errors, authentication errors, timeouts, general API errors, and rate limit errors, pointing toward upstream regional service degradation disproportionately impacting high-value enterprise traffic.

Immediate triage must begin with investigating the root cause of internal server errors and timeouts in the us-west and us-east regions to restore enterprise tier stability. Second, engineering needs to audit authentication and rate-limiting enforcement mechanisms to address the clustered AuthenticationError and RateLimitError occurrences. Finally, capacity and performance tuning should be applied to the embeddings and completions endpoints to bring their error rates back below the 2.0 percent healthy threshold.
