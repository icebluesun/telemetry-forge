Platform Health Summary:

Platform health is critical for October 20, 2026, with an overall error rate of 7.41 percent across 1,053 total requests from 343 daily active users. This represents a worsening from the previous day, October 19, which saw 1,555 requests from 497 daily active users and a 6.69 percent error rate. Geographically, us-east and us-west are severely impacted with error rates of 10.49 and 9.59 percent. Most notably, enterprise users are experiencing a catastrophic 38.24 percent error rate and a 259.0 millisecond p95 latency, while pro users face a 9.92 percent error rate and 169.2 millisecond p95 latency.

Immediate mitigation must prioritize the enterprise tier and the us-east and us-west regions to address the high concentration of timeouts and internal server errors. Engineering teams should investigate upstream timeout causes affecting completions and embeddings endpoints, particularly for enterprise workloads generating heavy latency. Finally, auth and rate-limiting configurations need urgent review to resolve AuthenticationError and RateLimitError spikes impacting high-value accounts before user churn accelerates.
