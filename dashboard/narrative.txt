Platform Health Summary:

The API platform is operating in a critical state with an overall error rate of 7.17 percent across 1,353 requests from 453 daily active users, representing a degradation from the previous day's error rate of 6.67 percent across 1,544 requests and 531 users. Average latency remained stable at 22.4 milliseconds with a p95 of 59.9 milliseconds, and 2.37 percent of requests were rate-limited while processing 1,486,312 tokens.

Issues are heavily concentrated among enterprise and pro tier users, who experienced severe error rates of 32.5 percent and 10.19 percent respectively, compared to 5.16 percent for free users. Geographically, us-east and us-west regions suffered the highest error rates at 10.58 percent and 8.56 percent, while endpoint errors peaked on embeddings at 8.47 percent, completions at 7.22 percent, and chat at 7.18 percent. The primary failure drivers were InternalServerError and TimeoutError, which together account for nearly half of the top-reported failures, likely pointing to downstream capacity or timeout constraints disproportionately affecting higher-volume and enterprise workloads.

Immediate remediation must prioritize investigating the infrastructure supporting enterprise tier accounts and the us-east region to identify the root cause of timeouts and internal server errors. Concurrently, engineering should review capacity limits and execution timeouts on the embeddings and completions endpoints to stabilize performance for high-traffic operations. Finally, authentication and rate-limiting configurations should be audited to address the secondary cluster of AuthenticationError and RateLimitError occurrences.
