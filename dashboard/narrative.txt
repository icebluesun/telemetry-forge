Platform Health Summary:

API platform health is currently critical as overall error rates remain elevated well above the 5.0 percent threshold. On 2026-11-17, the platform handled 1374 requests across 401 daily active users with an error rate of 6.48 percent, an average latency of 23.1 milliseconds, a p95 latency of 65.3 milliseconds, and 1.51 million tokens processed. This represents a slight negative day-over-day shift from 2026-11-16, where 1502 requests from 444 users resulted in a 6.32 percent error rate, 22.5 millisecond average latency, and 61.2 millisecond p95 latency.

Failures are heavily concentrated in specific segments, led by the enterprise and pro user tiers experiencing severe error rates of 12.7 percent and 9.42 percent respectively, alongside high p95 latencies reaching 182.6 and 143.6 milliseconds. Regionally, us-west and us-east are struggling most with error rates of 8.93 percent and 8.42 percent, while the embeddings endpoint is the worst performing at 8.65 percent errors. The primary drivers behind these metrics are InternalServerErrors at 21 occurrences, APIErrors at 18, TimeoutErrors at 17, and RateLimitError at 16, which account for 2.47 percent of total traffic.

Immediate remediation should focus on investigating the root cause of the InternalServerErrors and TimeoutErrors affecting high-value enterprise and pro tiers in the us-west and us-east regions. Secondarily, engineering needs to audit the embeddings endpoint and infrastructure capacity to address its 8.65 percent error rate, while also evaluating rate-limiting thresholds to protect degraded services. Finally, monitoring should be tightened on the chat endpoint due to its elevated p95 latency of 101.2 milliseconds despite stable traffic volumes.
